In the Sheet, open the Master tab, select the header row plus every row of the current month, copy, and paste below. Column order does not matter — columns are matched by name (DATE OF REFERRAL, GCS CATEGORY, EXPIRED, BRAIN DEATH DECLARED, FAMILY APPROACHED, FAMILY CONSENT, ORGANS DONATED, FINAL OUTCOME / STATUS). Tab-separated or CSV both work.

Rows are counted, never copied — no case detail reaches the sheet, and the NAME column is discarded on read, per the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Week runs Monday to Sunday around the sheet date; the month is the sheet date's month; the daily funnel below takes only POTENTIAL cases dated on the sheet date. Nothing is sent anywhere; parsing happens in this page.
